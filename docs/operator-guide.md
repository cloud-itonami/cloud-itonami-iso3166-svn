# Operator Guide

## First Deployment

1. Confirm the client's incorporation/legal-entity status is complete
   (route to `cloud-itonami-M6910` or local counsel first if not).
2. Register the client's intake: business type, target public function,
   prior filing history in Slovenia if any.
3. Run the advisor in read-only mode against e-JN (Elektronsko javno
   naročanje, ejn.gov.si), operated by Ministrstvo za notranje zadeve
   in javno upravo (Ministry of the Interior and Public
   Administration) under the Zakon o javnem naročanju (Public
   Procurement Act).
4. Compare the checklist against the client's current documentation:
   AJPES Poslovni register extract, davčna številka assignment (FURS,
   including via Form DR-04 for a foreign legal entity before it
   commences business activity in Slovenia), and e-JN bidder /
   economic-operator registration.
5. If the client's own Slovenian market presence was established by a
   foreign investor acquiring >= 10% of the capital/voting rights of
   an existing Slovenian company in an activity touching a listed risk
   factor, confirm the Zakon o spodbujanju investicij (ZSInv)
   notification to Ministrstvo za gospodarstvo, delo in šport was
   filed before proceeding.
6. Enable gated filing-draft assistance once the Market-Entry
   Compliance Governor contract is trusted; actual submission always
   requires human sign-off.

## Minimum Production Controls

- client-owned data store for business/tax registration documents
- clear provenance (official portal/regulation citation) for every
  requirement surfaced
- approval workflow for any e-JN registration or filing submission
- named referral relationship with Slovenian-licensed counsel or a
  registered agent for anything beyond checklist/draft assistance
- monthly audit export

## Certification

Certified operators must prove data provenance, audit traceability, that
automated actions cannot bypass the Market-Entry Compliance Governor, and
a working referral relationship with Slovenian-licensed counsel or a
registered agent for whatever licensed representation the law of
Slovenia requires for actual public-procurement filings.
